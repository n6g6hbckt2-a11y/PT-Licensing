PT Licensing

PT Licensing provides premises and alcohol licensing support to pubs, bars and restaurants in Horsham and across East and West Sussex, Kent, Surrey and Hampshire. We help hospitality operators apply for, vary and protect the licences their business depends on — guiding you through the Licensing Act 2003 process, local authority requirements and the responsible authorities so you can focus on running your venue.

Whether you are opening a new site, taking over an existing one or responding to a challenge to your current licence, we offer clear, practical advice and handle the paperwork, consultation and hearings on your behalf. (Placeholder introduction — to be replaced with final copy.)
